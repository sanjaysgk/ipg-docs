## What this page will cover

| Step | Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 24 | `gatk IndexFeatureFile` | Index `_unmasked.vcf` and `_indel.vcf` |
| 25 | `gatk FastaAlternateReferenceMaker` | Build two alt-ref FASTAs (unmasked + indel) |
| 26 | **`revert_headers`** (kescull C) | Restore chr names that `FastaAlternateReferenceMaker` rewrote |
| 27 | **`gff3sort.pl`** (third-party Perl) | `--chr_order original` on `prefix.combined.gtf` |
| 28 | **`alt_liftover`** (kescull C) | Lift GTF coordinates onto the alt-ref genomes (run twice) |
| 29 | `gffread` | Three runs: unmasked-tx, indel-tx, reference-tx → transcriptome FASTAs |
| 30 | **`triple_translate`** (kescull C) | 3-frame translation of each transcriptome → protein FASTA |
| 31 | **`squish`** (kescull C) | Merge + dedup the three protein DBs → **`<sample>_cryptic.fasta`** (the deliverable) |

The `<sample>_cryptic.fasta` is the input to MS database search engines (PEAKS or compatible). Downstream classification of the identified peptides is the scope of the planned [`ipg-origins` sister pipeline](/background/related-work).
