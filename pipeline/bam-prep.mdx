## What this page will cover

| Step | Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | STAR 2.7.9a (second pass) | Variant-calling-grade alignment, separate output dir |

| 8 | `gatk FastqToSam` | Build unmapped BAM with read groups |
| 9 | `gatk MergeBamAlignment` | Merge aligned + unmapped BAMs |
| 10 | `gatk MarkDuplicates` | Mark PCR / optical duplicates (`--READ_NAME_REGEX null`) |
| 11 | `gatk SplitNCigarReads` | RNA-seq specific: split reads at N's in CIGAR |
| 12 | `gatk ValidateSamFile` | Sanity check the prepared BAM |
